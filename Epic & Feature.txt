------------------------------------------------------
Epic 1: User Management

Features:

Registration (email/phone signup, password management)

Login (secure authentication, forgot password)

Profile Management (personal info, profile photo, update password)

Epic 2: Bus Management

Features:

Bus Details (add/edit/delete bus, timings, routes, pricing, type – AC/Non-AC, sleeper/seater)

Photos & Documents (upload bus images, RC, Insurance, Permit, approval workflow)

Seat Layout (configure seats, mark reserved/unavailable seats)

Epic 3: Search & Booking

Features:

Smart Search Engine (filter by source, destination, date, bus type, price range, seat availability, rating)

Seat Selection (interactive seat map, multiple seats, real-time availability)

Booking Confirmation (ticket generation, booking history, cancellation option)

Epic 4: Payments & Notifications

Features:

Payments (integrate gateway, multiple modes – UPI, card, wallet, net banking)

Payment Confirmation (success/failure flow, receipt generation)

Notifications (email/SMS/app alerts for booking confirmation, payment receipt, reminders, cancellation alerts)

Epic 5: Admin & Reporting

Features:

Admin Dashboard (overview of buses, bookings, users, revenue, documents pending approval)

Reports (daily/weekly/monthly bookings, occupancy rate, top routes, revenue trends)

Monitoring & Control (approve buses/documents, manage cancellations, handle disputes)
